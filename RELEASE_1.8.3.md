# LorMusic 1.8.3 · Build 96

## Video and audio

- Added a full-screen video-to-audio converter under **Tracks → ⋮ → Video to audio**. Preview video with sound before exporting to **WAV (PCM)** or **M4A (AAC)**. AAC export offers 96, 160, and 256 kbps options.
- Edit metadata, lyrics, and cover art before exporting in either format.
- Open videos directly from your file manager with **Open with → LorMusic**. Videos play in a separate window over the app they were opened from.

## Achievements

- Added **41 achievements** for listening, building your library, using music services, customizing the app, and discovering hidden features.
- The Achievements screen shows your overall progress, collapsible categories, and progress toward individual goals.
- Achievements are off by default. When enabled, new unlocks appear as in-app notifications with a trophy icon.

## Appearance and navigation

- Redesigned the **Minimalism** app icon and its themed variant.
- Replaced the app icon list with a swipeable carousel. Tap the checkmark to apply the icon you’re previewing.
- Redesigned accent previews as three-tone swatches and added **Cherry Wine**, **Smoky Indigo**, and **Burnt Amber**.
- Made the search bars in YouTube Music and SoundCloud shorter and rounder.
- Added **Predictive Back** as an opt-in beta feature on Android 14 and later.

## System & Updates

- Settings are now always organized into categories.
- Redesigned **System & Updates** around your active app icon, with a matching full-screen gradient and a plain, centered header without blur.
- Added a GitHub shortcut with a confirmation dialog that shows the repository URL before opening it.
- Added an offline viewer for the full **GNU GPL v3.0** license.
- Localized the new System & Updates labels and dialogs in English, Ukrainian, German, and Polish.
