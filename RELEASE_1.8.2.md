# LorMusic 1.8.2 · Liquid Glass Update

## Liquid Glass

- Added a dedicated **Liquid Glass** settings section with an on/off switch, a strength slider, and a live navigation preview.
- Set the default strength to **15%** and added a styled reset button. Existing custom strength settings carry over when updating.
- Reworked bottom navigation with elastic tab movement, optical refraction, and an animated selection indicator.
- Added stronger, spring-loaded drag feedback to circular glass buttons. Glass switches follow your finger and use a green track when enabled.
- Updated glass sliders, overflow menus, and sorting menus. Menus open on tap and keep their glass background throughout the animation.
- Applied Liquid Glass to the player’s top controls, with standard blur when Liquid Glass is disabled.

## Library and playback

- Added a **Hidden Tracks** system playlist that appears when you hide your first track. You can play tracks from it or restore them to your main feed.
- Fixed SoundCloud browsing and playback, including recovery from expired sessions and selection of playable stream formats.

## Navigation and appearance

- Moved the search button permanently to the bottom navigation in the local library, YouTube Music, and SoundCloud. Removed the placement option from Settings and onboarding.
- Added the optional **LorMusic Pulse** launcher icon, a blue **L** with a play symbol.
- Redesigned the **Minimalism** icon with a blue shape and a double music note. Updated its adaptive and monochrome layers.
- The **About** row now displays the app version directly in Settings.
